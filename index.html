    How to layer

    Put the shortest chain at the base of the neck, a mid strand on the collarbone, and one pendant lower. Mix a fine trace chain with something heavier so the lengths don’t tangle.

    Metals

    Yellow gold plate over brass, and sterling silver. Waterproof pieces are labelled on the card and are made to be worn in the shower and the sea. Pearls prefer to come off for swimming.
